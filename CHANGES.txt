Version 1.0.0 | 2026-May-31
 - Initial public release of the Custom Braille Embosser.
 - 10 fully 3D-printed parts: Body Base, Body Top, Embosser Holder A1/A2, Counter Holder B1/B2, Grip Extension Bottom/Top, Handle Connector, Handle Grip.
 - No off-the-shelf parts, no fasteners, no springs, no glue: snap-fit assembly only.
 - Designed in Autodesk Fusion 360; source `.f3z`, neutral `.step`, and `.obj`+`.mtl` exports included.
 - Sliced print profiles provided for Bambu Lab X1C: `Braille Embosser Assembly v1.0 (0.2 Layer Height).3mf` (all 10 device parts; 0.2 mm layer height) and `Embossing and Counter Cylinder High Quality.3mf` (cylinder pair only; 0.12 mm layer height with Arachne walls).
 - Compatible with cylindrical embossing-plate / counter-plate pairs produced by the companion projects `braille-cylinder-stl-generator` (web-based, primary) and `braille-cylinder-stl-generator-openscad` (OpenSCAD, alternative).
 - Two officially supported customizer paths:
   - PRIMARY - Web-based customizer (recommended for most users): https://braille-cylinder-stl-generator.vercel.app
     auto-translates English to Grade 1 / Grade 2 braille across 50+ language tables; STL generation runs entirely in your browser; no install.
   - ALTERNATIVE - OpenSCAD offline customizer (vendored locally for offline use):
     `Build_Files/3D_Printing_Files/Embossing_Plates_Customizer/OpenSCAD_Offline_Alternative/`
     copy of `braille-cylinder-stl-generator-openscad` v2.2.1 (2026-05-30); PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0.
 - Verified for 0.3 mm and 0.4 mm card stock; example plate pairs included for both thicknesses in `Build_Files/3D_Printing_Files/Embossing_Plates_Examples/`.
 - Three video tutorials: (1) generating and printing custom plates, (2) assembling the device, (3) operating the completed device. Hosted on YouTube; the .mp4 files are too large for git (368-705 MB each, past GitHub's 100 MB limit), so `Video_Tutorials/` publishes the .srt captions, the per-step assembly screenshots, and a transcript-derived written walkthrough instead:
   - Video 1: https://youtu.be/m71VNZq28MI
   - Video 2: https://youtu.be/x-uPNtT2Gqk
   - Video 3: https://youtu.be/77iXvJbkXh4
 - Documentation: README, OKH manifest, Bill of Materials, Maker Guide, User Guide, Quickstart Guide, Summary, Design Rationale.
